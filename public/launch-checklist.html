Finish the Operating Agreement

Lock in the key decisions BEFORE any money moves

0 / 5
Decide: Ownership Split & Contributions
You and Heather must agree on: Ownership % each (50/50?), Mark's cash contribution, Heather's cash contribution, non-cash contributions (relationships, contacts, labor), time commitment. Write these down now — most partnership conflicts start here.
CRITICAL✓ Free
Decide: Decision-Making Authority
Agree before signing: Can either partner sign supplier/buyer contracts alone? Spending threshold (e.g., over $5,000 needs both signatures)? Can either hire employees or rent a warehouse unilaterally? Can either bring in a third partner? Set clear boundaries now.
CRITICAL✓ Free
Decide: Profit Distribution & Draws
How do profits split? Does it match ownership % or different? How often do you take distributions (monthly, quarterly, annual)? How much stays in the business vs. gets paid out? As a partnership, you'll each pay self-employment taxes on your full share — discuss this.
CRITICAL✓ Free
Decide: Buyout Terms & Deadlock Resolution
Buyout formula if either partner wants out? Can the departing partner force a sale of the whole LLC? 50/50 deadlock tie-breaker (arbitration? one party forces buyout?). Discuss NOW, not when you're angry.
CRITICAL✓ Free
Draft & Sign Operating Agreement
Use SCORE.org template or Claude draft. Customize based on your decisions. Both sign & date two copies (each keeps one). PA does NOT require filing, but you MUST have it signed before revenue hits the bank. Then take to PA attorney for quick review (~$300–500).

Sign Up for Registered Agent / CROP Service

Do this BEFORE filing — your home address will NOT go on public record

Sign up with registered agent service (e.g., Northwest Registered Agent)
The Certificate of Organization requires a registered office address. If you don't use an agent, your home address becomes permanently public. Northwest Registered Agent (~$125/yr) is a CROP in PA and often helps file the LLC for you. Get their address — you'll use it on the filing.

File Certificate of Organization with PA

This legally creates your LLC. Takes ~7–10 business days online.

File Articles of Organization online via PA Business One-Stop Hub
Go to PA Business One-Stop Hub and file online (15 minutes). Use the registered agent address from Step 3. Fee: $125. You'll receive a confirmation email with your Certificate PDF — save it permanently. You'll need it for bank account, EIN application, and all contracts. Expedited 24-hour service: additional $100.

5

Get Your EIN from IRS

Free, takes 10 minutes, available immediately. Needed for bank account & contracts.

Apply for EIN at IRS.gov
Go to irs.gov and use their online application (5 minutes). Your EIN is issued instantly on screen. Write it down and store it in multiple safe locations. The IRS will NOT retrieve it if you lose it — they'll only mail it to address on file (weeks). As a two-member LLC, you'll be classified as a partnership by default — that's fine.

8

Get Insurance & Confirm PA Regulatory

General Liability + Contingent Cargo + E&O (waste brokers regret skipping this)

0 / 3
Get General Liability + Contingent Cargo + E&O Insurance
General Liability: $1M/$2M coverage (manufacturers require this before signing). Contingent Cargo: covers you if a carrier damages/loses a load and their insurance doesn't cover it (THIS is what waste brokers skip and regret). Errors & Omissions: covers claims related to your advice or service. For small brokerage expect ~$500–900/yr for GL, ~$400–800/yr for cargo. Get instant quotes from Next Insurance or Hiscox. Heather's carrier contacts can recommend an agent who knows waste/freight. Once policies active, request COI (Certificate of Insurance) — you'll email it to every prospect. If asked to be "Additional Insured," contact insurer; typically free.
BEFORE FIRST DEAL💵 ~$1,300–1,700/yr
Next Insurance ↗ Hiscox ↗
Call PA DEP: Confirm waste transporter & regulatory requirements
PA Department of Environmental Protection: 717-783-2300. Anyone physically transporting residual/municipal waste may need a Waste Transporter Permit. If you only arrange transportation (don't haul yourself), different rules apply. This 20-minute call clarifies exactly what applies to your model BEFORE your first load moves. Also confirm plastics you handle qualify as non-hazardous under EPA Subtitle D. Penalties for non-compliance far outweigh permit cost.
BEFORE FIRST LOAD✓ Free call
PA DEP Solid Waste ↗ EPA Regulations ↗
Engage a CPA for tax planning & annual returns
Contact 2–3 CPAs in Boyertown/Lansdale area who specialize in small business & LLC partnerships. Your multi-member LLC files Form 1065 (partnership return) annually by March 15; each partner gets a K-1 showing profit/loss share. Discuss S-corp election strategy (could save self-employment taxes if substantial profit). Get quotes for annual 1065 ($500–1,000) and consulting ($200–300). Have CPA review operating agreement for tax implications.
